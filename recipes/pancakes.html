Pancakes

Description

Make easy and fast pancakes. They are soft and flavoursome. So get your pan ready and follow along

  • Milk
  • Flour
  • Egg
  • Sugar
  • Syrup
  • Butter
  1. Put egg, 1/2 cup of flour, 1/2 cup of milk and 1 spoon of sugar in a bowl
  2. Mix it until you have a doug
  3. Butter the pan
  4. Leave the pancake for 1 minute on the pan
  5. Serve with syrup and butter
