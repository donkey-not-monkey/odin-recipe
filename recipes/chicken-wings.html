Chicken wings

Delicious grilled chicken wings

Cut the wings into pieces and wash them properly. After that mix them up in the barbecue sauce and let them sit. Heat up your grilled to 215 degrees celcius and put the wings there. Grill them until the skin is crisp.

  • Chicken wings
  • Barbecue sauce
  • Sesame seeds
  • Salt
  • Beer
  1. Cut the wings
  2. Wash the pieces
  3. Mix them in a bowl with barbecue sauce
  4. Heat the grill
  5. Grill the wings until the skin is crisp
  6. Sprinkle sesame seeds on top
  7. Serve with cold drinks
